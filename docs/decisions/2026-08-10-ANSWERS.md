## 1. When the v3 mock and your eye disagree, which wins? → **A**, "can review later"

**The mock wins. UI-1 and UI-6 close.**
The header bar stays flush to the top of the main region, and the "Food cost trend" section stays uncarded on the page canvas.
Both are the v3 mock's own design, built faithfully, and the page reading card / not-card / card is the mock's rhythm rather than a defect.

⚠️ **"Can review later" is part of the answer and is recorded deliberately.**
The question's own "what would change it" line said a first-look reaction to a new layout is usually about unfamiliarity, while a week-two reaction is about the design.
He has taken A on that basis, not unconditionally.
**So if either of these comes back after he has lived with the screen, it is NOT a re-litigation and must not be answered with "decided 10 Aug".** It is the second signal the question asked for, and the answer then is C (keep the flush header, card the trend).

## 2. Header rule wider than the content, or the same width? → **A**

**Full-bleed.** The header's hairline runs the whole width of the main area, wider than it is now, as the mock draws it.
This closes UI-2 in the opposite direction to the way it was reported: the rule is currently too NARROW, not too wide.
The queued item *"The v3 screen header bar is not full-bleed"* is the one that survives; UI-2's "identical edges" AC is dead.
Consistent with 1A, which is the condition the recommendation was given under.

## 3. "Last change" on unchanged products → **C**

**A dash (—), not the word "steady" and not blank.**
Deviates from the mock, which prints "steady", and that is fine: the mock's fixture never shows more than three at once, and Scoopy's catalogue shows fifteen in a row.
A dash is what every other "nothing here" cell in the app already renders, so this is the consistent choice rather than a new one.
**Applies to Ingredients as well as Products** — they share the wording and it is one function.

## 4. Colour-coded KPI figures on the Dashboard → **A**

**Keep them.** The headline figures stay coloured against the target.
Every other figure on that screen is anchored to the target, and that consistency is what makes the colour mean anything.
UI-15 closes.
Note this also re-confirms, for the third time, that a "vs last month" delta is not wanted (v98 deleted it, 9 Aug 2026 declined it, and it was the obvious mechanism for option B).

## 5. Three accent hues in the trend section → **B**

**Restyle the range control so the active option is not orange.**
The markers stay orange: they mean "you did this", the line means "here is where you stand against target", and the two must not share a hue.
The range control is a button rather than data, so it is the one that stops competing.
UI-16 closes as a decision and becomes a small build.
