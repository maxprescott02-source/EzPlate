The six empty states speak in two voices

A new café walking Products → Ingredients → Plates → Menu meets report, invite, invite, report. The bodies and the buttons are all fine and consistent — it is only the titles that disagree. Batch 214 read all six together, which is what the queue item asked for and what nobody had done.

ScreenTitle todayGrammar
Products“No products yet.”status
Ingredients“Name your first ingredient”invitation
Plates“Cost your first plate”invitation
Menu — no menus“No menus yet.”status
Menu — empty menu“Nothing on this menu yet.”status
Dashboard — Dig in“Nothing to rank yet.”status

The split is not random, which is why this is a choice and not a bug. Five of the six fit a rule: a screen where you make the thing, with one obvious action, invites; a screen showing something derived from other screens reports. The one that breaks it is “No menus yet.” — one action, and it still reports.

A — change one line

Rule: one obvious action → invite; everything else → report. Products keeps reporting because it offers two actions and has no single verb.

Products“No products yet.”
Ingredients“Name your first ingredient”
Plates“Cost your first plate”
Menu — none“Create your first menu”
Menu — empty“Nothing on this menu yet.”
Dig in“Nothing to rank yet.”

B — every screen you create on invites (recommended)

Rule: you make it here → invite; it comes from elsewhere → report. Two tiers, and the tier a new screen belongs to is obvious.

C — everything reports

Rule: titles state what is there; bodies and buttons say what to do.
